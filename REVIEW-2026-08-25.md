## 3. New findings

Severity: **3 HIGH · 9 MEDIUM · ~15 LOW · ~10 INFO.** No CRITICAL.

### HIGH

**R2-H1 — Asset identities are NOT stable across JSON persistence (invalid UTF-8 silently rewritten)**
`internal/asset`: `url.go:275–350` (`escapeRawQuery` escapes only space/#/&/=), `parameter.go`, `evidence.go`, `secret_candidate.go` accept raw non-ASCII/invalid UTF-8 into identity-bearing fields; `json.Marshal` (cache put, `cache/cache.go:266`) replaces invalid bytes with U+FFFD → decoded asset recomputes a DIFFERENT identity. **Independently reproduced by lead reviewer** (in-package probe, tree restored clean): ID before RT `url:https://example.com/?q=\xff\xfe`, after RT U+FFFD-substituted; UNSTABLE=true. Consequence: cache round-trips split one observation into two identities — dedup misses, Merge fails "identities differ", identity-derived keys go stale; stored records mutate under persistence with no marker (§0.6-adjacent at THE dedup boundary). Fix: reject non-UTF-8 at validated ingestion points + percent-encode ≥0x80 in `escapeRawQuery`; regression-test ID() invariance across json.Marshal/Unmarshal; add FuzzAssetJSONRoundTrip.

**R2-H2 — v2.0 JS pack emits high-priority XSS/pollution findings from URL filename substrings**
`internal/detect/packs/js/domxss.go:66–67` fires DOM-XSS on `Contains(url,"xss")||Contains(url,"dom")` — wisdom.js, kingdom, and DOMPurify (a sanitizer) all fire; `prototypepollution.go:66–67` fires on `"proto"` (protobuf.js); `postmessage.go:66–67` on `"postmessage"`. Findings ship Priority high / Confidence 0.92, are validated, CACHED (key includes snapshot fingerprint → FPs persist warm), and propagated into reports indistinguishable from content-derived detections. Commit message implies parse-tree analysis that does not exist; tests encode the substring mapping as spec. **Independently confirmed by lead reviewer.** Fix: cut the three rules until script bodies are analyzable, or rename/re-grade honestly as url_substring heuristics (CategoryInformation, ≤0.5 confidence, whole-segment tokens).

**R2-H3 — techintel cookie matching matches VALUES, contradicting the fingerprint data-model contract; value path persists raw cookie material into evidence**
`internal/techintel/analyze.go:738–745`: IndicatorCookie is name-OR-value; on the value branch the FULL observed cookie value (≤256 B) becomes `Evidence.Value`, persisted into `storedTech.Evidence` and reports. Contracts disagree verbatim: `fingerprints/doc.go:46` + `types.go:22–23` document NAME-only; `techintel/doc.go:69` says "name OR value". Live entry `{Cookie,"session",Weight:.5}` (`frameworks.go:223`) means `Set-Cookie: prefs=returning:/session/hub` yields flask @ Medium — plus session-token-shaped strings routed into durable output. **Independently confirmed by lead reviewer.** Fix: restrict to name matching (matches every DB entry's authoring intent), or make value-matching an explicit opt-in with redaction on the value path.
