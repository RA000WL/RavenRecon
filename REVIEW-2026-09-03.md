## 4. Dispositions (investigated, deliberately not changed)

- **Reflection `Absent` evidence kept.** Emitting only positives would kill the triage DROP path (the feature's core value). Volume is bounded (16 params/URL × 512 URLs), deterministic, merge-capped with `evidence_truncated` honesty. Reviewer concern recorded; design stands.
- **Priority `jsSize`/`methods` joins rarely hit** (script URL ≠ page URL). True but harmless (zero fields = today's behavior); methods fire on probe-shape endpoints. Kept; noted as future work alongside endpoint-identity correlation.
- **Detect `cloneContextForRule` shares inner finding slices.** Read-only by contract, pinned for Metadata; deep-clone cost unjustified. Kept.
- **Takeover uncached by design; reflection cached.** Freshness-critical claims re-verify every run; candidate sets tiny. Kept.
- **JS predicate precision (string-contains on real bodies).** Redesigning without FP field measurements risks churn/regressions; filed as NEW-123 with a measurement gate. Not rebuilt blind.
